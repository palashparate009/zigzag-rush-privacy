Privacy Policy for ZigZag Rush

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Developer: Palash Parate
Contact: palashparate009@gmail.com

Overview

This policy explains how information is handled when you play ZigZag Rush. The game has no account system and does not operate a developer-owned server for gameplay progress.

Information stored on your device

ZigZag Rush stores gameplay information locally, including high score, gem balance, unlocked and selected cosmetic items, daily challenge and mission progress, tutorial status, music and haptic settings, and your advertising choice. You can remove this local information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Advertising and third-party processing

The game uses Unity LevelPlay mediation, Unity Ads, and ironSource Ads to provide optional rewarded advertisements and occasional interstitial advertisements. These services may process device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device and app information, ad views and interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention or security signals. The information actually processed can depend on your location, device settings, consent choice, and the advertisement served.

These providers may use permitted information to deliver and measure advertisements, limit repeated ads, detect fraud and abuse, maintain service security, diagnose reliability, and personalize ads where allowed and consented to.

For more information, review Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your advertising choices

On first launch, the game lets you choose personalized advertising or contextual advertising. You can revisit this choice from the Privacy option in the game menu. You can also use the privacy controls presented inside Unity advertisements and your Android device's advertising privacy settings. Ads may still appear when personalization is declined.

Sharing and international processing

Information may be shared with Unity and the advertising, measurement, security, and demand partners involved in serving an ad. These providers may process information in countries other than your own and apply the safeguards described in their policies. Palash Parate does not sell gameplay progress stored locally on your device.

Retention and deletion

Local gameplay information remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the game. Advertising providers retain information according to their own policies. For questions or requests concerning the game, email palashparate009@gmail.com. Requests relating to information controlled by Unity can also be made using the privacy options and contact methods in Unity's policy.

Children

ZigZag Rush is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you are below the age at which you can consent to personalized advertising in your country, use contextual ads and involve a parent or guardian where required.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the game. No method of electronic processing or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game's features, advertising providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version.
