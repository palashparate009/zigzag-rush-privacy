Privacy Policy for ZigZag Rush

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Developer: Palash Parate
Contact: palashparate009@gmail.com

Overview

This policy explains how information is handled when you play ZigZag Rush. Google Play Games and Firebase Authentication may be used to create a game account, and Firebase Cloud Firestore may store gameplay progress for cloud save.

Information stored on your device

ZigZag Rush stores gameplay information locally, including high score, gem balance, unlocked and selected cosmetic items, daily and weekly challenge and mission progress, tutorial status, music and haptic settings, and service configuration. You can remove this local information by using Delete Account, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the game.

Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect gameplay and app-interaction information such as game starts, scores, collected gem totals, advertising outcomes, purchase outcomes, app version, device information, and approximate location derived from IP address. This information is used to understand game performance, improve gameplay, measure engagement, and diagnose problems. The game does not send your name, email address, phone number, or purchase transaction identifier to analytics.

Analytics is part of the game service and is enabled automatically. The in-game Privacy screen links to this policy.

For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Optional gem purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google processes payment and account information; ZigZag Rush does not receive or store your payment-card details. The game receives the purchased product, purchase result, localized price, and currency so it can deliver gems and measure purchase outcomes. Purchase outcome events may be recorded in Google Analytics for Firebase as described above.

Advertising and third-party processing

The game uses Unity LevelPlay mediation, Unity Ads, ironSource Ads, and Google AdMob to provide banner advertisements, optional rewarded advertisements, and occasional interstitial advertisements. These services may process device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device and app information, ad views and interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention or security signals. The information actually processed can depend on your location, device settings, consent choice, and the advertisement served.

These providers may use permitted information to deliver and measure contextual advertisements, limit repeated ads, detect fraud and abuse, maintain service security, and diagnose reliability. The game does not enable personalized advertising through its own controls.

For more information, review Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Google's Advertising Policies and Technologies.

Service configuration

Anonymous gameplay analytics and contextual advertising are enabled automatically and do not have separate in-game on/off controls. You can still use privacy controls provided by advertising partners and your Android device. Ads may appear while you use the game.

Sharing and international processing

Information may be shared with Google, Firebase, Unity, and the advertising, measurement, security, and demand partners used by the game. These providers may process information in countries other than your own and apply the safeguards described in their policies. Palash Parate does not sell gameplay progress.

Account and data deletion

Delete inside the game: Open ZigZag Rush, go to the main menu, select Delete Account, and confirm. This deletes the player's Firebase Authentication account, Firestore cloud-save document, and local game progress.

Request deletion without the game: Email palashparate009@gmail.com with the subject ZigZag Rush Account Deletion Request. Send the request from the Google account used for Google Play Games and include your Play Games gamer name. Never send your password. Additional information may be requested only when necessary to verify account ownership. Verified requests are normally completed within 30 days.

The deletion removes the ZigZag Rush Firebase account and associated cloud gameplay progress. Information stored only on a device cannot be deleted remotely; clear the app's storage or uninstall the game to remove it. Purchase records, security or fraud-prevention records, and information controlled independently by Google Play Games, Google, Unity, or advertising providers may be retained where required by law or under those providers' policies.

Children

ZigZag Rush is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The game requests contextual rather than personalized advertising.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the game. No method of electronic processing or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game's features, advertising providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version.
